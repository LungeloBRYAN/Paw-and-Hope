About Paws & Hope

A community-built non-profit, run by people who simply could not walk past an animal in need.

Our History

Paws & Hope Animal Rescue was founded in 2015 by a small group of volunteer foster carers who kept encountering the same problem: far more abandoned and at-risk animals than any single shelter in the area could handle. What began as an informal WhatsApp group coordinating foster placements has since grown into a registered non-profit organisation running a small shelter facility, an active network of foster homes, monthly mobile adoption days, and a community sterilisation drive.

Today, Paws & Hope is run by a small core team supported by more than 120 volunteers, and works closely with local vets, pet food suppliers and corporate sponsors to keep our programmes running.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To rescue, rehabilitate and rehome abandoned and at-risk companion animals, and to reduce animal homelessness through community education and accessible sterilisation.

Vision: A community where every companion animal has a safe, loving, permanent home.
