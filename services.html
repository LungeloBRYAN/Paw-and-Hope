Our Services & Programmes

Three ways Paws & Hope works to get animals safely into loving homes and keep them there.

How We Help

Adoption

A structured, supportive process that matches animals with the right home, not just any home.

Foster Programme

Temporary homes that give animals a safe, calm space while they wait to be adopted.

Community Outreach

Low-cost sterilisation drives and pet-care education to prevent homelessness before it starts.

How Adoption Works

Every adoption follows the same four steps, designed to be simple for you and safe for the animal.

  1. Submit an enquiry

    Complete our enquiry form, telling us a little about your home, household and experience with pets.

  2. Meet & greet

    We arrange a visit so you can spend time with the animal at the shelter or their current foster home.

  3. Home check

    A volunteer visits your home (or does a short video call) to make sure it's a safe, suitable environment.

  4. Welcome home

    Once approved, you'll sign an adoption agreement, pay a small adoption fee that covers sterilisation and vaccinations, and take your new companion home.

Foster Carer Programme

Fostering gives an animal a safe, home-based environment to recover, grow in confidence, or simply wait for adoption without spending that time in a shelter kennel. Paws & Hope covers food and veterinary costs for all foster animals.

What fostering involves

  • Providing a safe indoor space, food and daily attention for a foster animal.
  • Attending a short orientation session before your first placement.
  • Bringing the animal to scheduled vet checks and monthly mobile adoption days.
  • Fostering periods typically range from two weeks to three months.

Community Outreach & Sterilisation Drives

Prevention is at the heart of reducing animal homelessness. Our monthly outreach days bring free and low-cost sterilisation, deworming and basic vet checks directly to under-resourced communities, alongside responsible pet-ownership education for families and schools.
